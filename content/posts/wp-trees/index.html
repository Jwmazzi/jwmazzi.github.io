Wild Potomac

I produced a series of maps for Wild Potomac to support a grant that funding tree planting within targeted areas in Brunswick.

Below are the overview maps I put together. They were built in QGIS and communicate the planting sites that are in or out of the various zones Wild Potomac was targeting.

51 of the 60 Google Form responses were selected as potential planting sites.
