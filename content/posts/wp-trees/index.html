Wild Potomac

Through Bird City Brunswick, I've been working with Wild Potomac on a grant that will fund tree plantings across the city.

Below are the overview maps I put together. They were built in QGIS and communicate the planting sites that are in or out of the various zones Wild Potomac was targeting.

51 of the 60 Google Form responses were selected as potential planting sites.




Simple Tools

This is the second project in the past couple months where I've really spent time figuring out my baseline FOSS4G tools.

Having built a print map for Springfest with QGIS and Inkscape, I wasn't starting from scratch for this project with Wild Potomac. QGIS continues to be excellent for quickly generating maps. This time I didn't use Inkscape and stuck with exporting simple layouts from QGIS. I'm still in the process of figuring out how much I want to use PostGIS through Docker in my work. Since this project was relatively simple, working out of a single GeoPackage and doing a mix of manual work in QGIS and writing some Python code was good enough.
