# Mapping with Wild Potomac
Bird City Brunswick is helping [Wild Potomac](https://www.wildpotomac.org/) with a grant that will plant trees across Brunswick at no cost to the homeowners who have responded to the survey.

Below are the overview maps I put together on Saturday morning. We will get these shopped up and included into the grant documentation. Publishing these via the Bird City Brunswick ArcGIS Online account may not work because of how it is currently licensed. Standing up infrastructure with GeoServer and using QGIS for stylig is the goal next I think.
