# Previously on E4 . . .
E4 levels up!

Rue suggests that we take a peek in the Guild Hall before heading back out to The Caves of Chaos.

On the way to the caves, we find Haldryck being remarkably suspect. He claims to have been hunting, but why would someone with nothing to hide offer us gold to keep something so simple secret?

We find a new bit of the caves to explore. The entrance to this section is covered with runes and Rue tries to communicate with something that we hear roaming within the darkness. Whatever it was, definitely not interested in chatting. 

Inside, we find a summoning circle and a library full of dark texts. We take the Iron Tome because someone back at The Keep, perhaps Elandra, will find likely it useful. Definitely can't leave it around for the baddies to keep using.

We also stumble right into a Carrion Crawler. Yikes! Can our heroes escape an almost certain death? Will Loverr return in time to save our crew from a nasty demise in the depths of Chaos!? Find out next time!
