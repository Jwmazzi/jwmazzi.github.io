Springfest 2025

Bird City and Sustainable Brunswick were given a booth for Springfest this year. With the experience from Railroad Days last year, I think we pulled off a very successful event. Our goal was to give away native plants through a swap and get people to the Garden Potluck.

Barb ran a bird walk in the morning and helped out all day.

We told people about Bird City events, county rebate programs, and a tree giveaway with Wild Potomac.

Our booth was at the far eastern side of the event, near where they had the kids and petting zoo. It was excellent to have people come down, pick up a plant, and then carry all of that green back through the event.

Giving away this iris was the highlighty of my day.

Technology Stuff

For our last couple events, I used Canva to build marketing materials & flyers, printed everything at home, and bought some cheap frames for presentation. This year, I invested in a small Arzopa monitor so that we could show the website and bring up anything people might be curious about. Not once during the event did I take out my laptop or the extra monitor, so I am glad we had the map. I also cleaned up an old Amazon tablet so that people could fill out Neil's survey. This is another thing I didn't end up bringing and we just had a laminated flyer with the QR code.

Making the map was a good exercise and it was really helpful. But mostly we remembered to show people the website, invite them to the Community Garden, and give them a flyer (below) to the potluck we are planning in June.
