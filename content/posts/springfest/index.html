Springfest 2025

Bird City has a booth for Springfest and a bit of experience from doing Railroad Days last year. We want people to come to the Community Garden and help us with other volunteer efforts across town; e.g., kudzu management out at River's Edge Trails.

Neil & Noah chatting with locals at Railroad Days 2024

For our last couple events, I used Canva to build marketing materials & flyers, printed everything at home, and bought some cheap frames for presentation. This year, I invested in a small Arzopa monitor so that we can show the website and bring up anything people might be curious about. I also cleaned up an old Amazon tablet so that we can have people fill out Neil's survey. We can show people the website, invite them to the Community Garden, and give them a flyer (below) to the potluck we are planning in June.

Canva is fine, but I'm probably just using Inkscape from now on.
