# Springfest 2025
Bird City has a booth for Springfest and a bit of experience from doing Railroad Days last year. We want people to come to the Community Garden and help us with other volunteer efforts across town; e.g., kudzu management out at River's Edge Trails.

<img src="railroad.jpg" alt="RR" style=""/>
<h3>Neil & Noah chatting with locals at Railroad Days 2024</h3>

For our last couple events, I used Canva to build marketing materials & flyers, printed everything at home, and bought some cheap frames for presentation. This year, I invested in a small Arzopa monitor so that we can show the website and bring up anything people might be curious about. I also cleaned up an old Amazon tablet so that we can have people fill out [Neil's survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe-Cq82KN_hNPP0PV9zyb2EVJ9z0ARVHyneeNjRyLsjRHMlgw/viewform?fbclid=IwY2xjawKE6s9leHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETFGQ1RIaVVpR0k5OWpySGo2AR5laxPaYlyrUJfTmyphV7RO5j4rZaLIWu71lpvZarukbc9DMY9mZjUgA4ZSEw_aem_JieoZM4_1LAPuQFA_cK2ww). We can show people the [website](https://birdcitybrunswick.org/), invite them to the [Community Garden](https://birdcitybrunswick.org/community-garden), and give them a flyer (below) to the potluck we are planning in June.

# Plant Swap
Our garden is starting to take shape, which means we can contribute a bit this year. The jewelweed that Joe gave me last year came back. We've also been pulling maples out of our beds and planting them in a big kiddie pool.
