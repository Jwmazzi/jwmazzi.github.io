# Springfest 2025
Bird City and Sustainable Brunswick were given a booth for Springfest this year. With the experience from Railroad Days last year, I think we pulled off a very successful event. Our goal was to give away native plants through a swap and get people to the [Garden Potluck](https://birdcitybrunswick.org/events/potluck-picnic/).

<img src="freeplants.jpg" alt="Free Plants" />
<h3>Barb ran a bird walk in the morning and helped out all day.</h3>

<img src="chatting.jpg" alt="Chatting" />
<h3>We told people about Bird City events, county rebate programs, and a tree giveaway with Wild Potomac.</h3>

Our booth was at the far eastern side of the event, near where they had the kids and petting zoo. It was excellent to have people come down, pick up a plant, and then carry all of that green back through the event.

<img src="iris.jpg" alt="Iris" />
<h3>Giving away this iris was the highlighty of my day.</h3>

# Technology Stuff

For our last couple events, I used Canva to build marketing materials & flyers, printed everything at home, and bought some cheap frames for presentation. This year, I invested in a small Arzopa monitor so that we could show the website and bring up anything people might be curious about. Not once during the event did I take out my laptop or the extra monitor, so I am glad we had the map. I also cleaned up an old Amazon tablet so that people could fill out [Neil's survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe-Cq82KN_hNPP0PV9zyb2EVJ9z0ARVHyneeNjRyLsjRHMlgw/viewform?fbclid=IwY2xjawKE6s9leHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETFGQ1RIaVVpR0k5OWpySGo2AR5laxPaYlyrUJfTmyphV7RO5j4rZaLIWu71lpvZarukbc9DMY9mZjUgA4ZSEw_aem_JieoZM4_1LAPuQFA_cK2ww). This is another thing I didn't end up bringing and we just had a laminated flyer with the QR code.

Making the map was a good exercise and it was really helpful. But mostly we remembered to show people the [website](https://birdcitybrunswick.org/), invite them to the [Community Garden](https://birdcitybrunswick.org/community-garden), and give them a flyer (below) to the potluck we are planning in June.
